Nexpath — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 July 2026

Nexpath is a browser extension made by ParseOS. It is an AI coding assistant that shows workflow guidance while you use AI coding agents — a behaviour-guidance layer that surfaces short decision sessions between your prompts.

It runs only on the supported sites: Replit, Bolt.new, StackBlitz, and Lovable. Nexpath is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of them.

The short version: Nexpath has no backend server. We never receive your prompts, your API key, or your settings. Your key and settings stay in your browser, and your prompt text goes only to OpenAI — using your own API key, under your own OpenAI account.

What Nexpath handles

  • Prompt text — the messages you type into the supported AI coding agent sites.
  • Your OpenAI API key — which you enter yourself in the extension's options page.
  • Your settings — such as suggestion frequency and your selected role.

How it is used

  • Your prompt text and minimal surrounding context are sent to OpenAI, using your own API key, to determine which phase of your development workflow you are in and to generate a short suggestion.
  • Your API key and settings are stored locally in your browser (extension storage). Your key never leaves your browser except when it is sent to OpenAI to authenticate your own requests.

Who it is shared with

  • Only OpenAI, to provide the feature, under your own OpenAI account.
  • No one else. Nexpath has no backend server. ParseOS does not receive, store, sell, or share your prompts, your API key, or your settings. There is no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

Data retention

  • Prompt text is processed transiently to generate a suggestion. It is not retained by ParseOS.
  • Your key and settings remain in your browser until you remove the extension or clear them.

Your choices

  • You can view, change, or delete your API key and settings at any time on the options page.
  • You can disable Nexpath for an individual project from the popup.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes the data stored locally by Nexpath.

Children's privacy

Nexpath is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13.
